Training & Optimization

Two-Stage SFT & GRPO Optimization Probe: Hyperparameters, Rewards, and Curves

Beyond evaluation, we examine whether UAV-DualCog can serve as effective supervision to bridge the semantic–evidence and Agent–World dual-cognition gaps. We construct UAV-DualCog-Train from six scenes strictly disjoint from the evaluation benchmark (11,232 samples, 234 landmarks) and train an edge-deployable base model (Qwen3.5-4B) using a two-stage alignment pipeline: Multimodal Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT) followed by Joint Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO).

Stage 1 · Multimodal SFT

Stabilizing Output Structure & Spatial Coordination

Stage 1 applies Parameter-Efficient Fine-Tuning (PEFT / LoRA) to train the model to output strict JSON conforming to the dual-cognition task contracts while jointly predicting multiple-choice answers and landmark bounding boxes.

Reward Formulation

Multi-Objective Composite Reward Contract

The four training tasks span two difficulty levels and retain the benchmark's answer-and-BBox output contract. After SFT stabilizes structured responses, GRPO scores each sampled completion with three equally weighted rewards for answer-and-box parseability, semantic correctness, and spatial grounding:

R=Rformat+Ranswer+RbboxR = R_{\mathrm{format}} + R_{\mathrm{answer}} + R_{\mathrm{bbox}}R=Rformat​+Ranswer​+Rbbox​
  • Rformat ∈ {0, 1}: Returns 1 when both an answer and a four-coordinate box can be extracted, and 0 otherwise. The parser accepts JSON fields answer_option_id (A–H) and bbox_xyxy_norm, with labeled-text fallbacks Answer: or Option: and BBox: or Bounding Box:. Box corners are reordered and clipped to [0, 1]; when the largest absolute coordinate exceeds 1.5, coordinates are first divided by 1,000. This extraction-based reward supports the canonical JSON output contract without requiring strict JSON serialization.
  • Ranswer ∈ {0, 1}: Discrete 0/1 indicator testing whether the predicted option matches ground truth a*.
  • Rbbox = clip((GIoU(B̂, B*) + 1) / 2, 0, 1) ∈ [0, 1]: Generalized Intersection over Union supplies a continuous localization signal, rescaled to the same reward range as the other two terms. GIoU(B̂, B*) = IoU(B̂, B*) - (|C| - |B̂ ∪ B*|) / |C|, where C is the smallest enclosing bounding box. Missing or unparseable boxes receive zero reward; zero-area boxes also receive zero BBox reward.
